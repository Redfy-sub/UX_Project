Piano

Invented around 1700 in Padua, Italy, by Bartolomeo Cristofori. He replaced the harpsichord's plucking quill mechanism with a hammer action. This allowed musicians to control volume dynamics—playing soft (piano) and loud (forte)—by how hard they pressed the keys.
